Partial Prompt Taxonomy

Every case is defined by a minimal pair: the same prompt with a test image and a reference image, or the same image under two prompts. The study measures how human ratings move between the two. Per sample, annotators give a 1–5 match rating, flag unwanted content with a click on its location, and report their confidence.
