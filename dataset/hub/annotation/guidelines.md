# Annotator guidelines

You will see a short text and an image. Imagine you typed the text into an image generator and got this image back.

Judge only **how well the image matches what the text asks for**. Do not judge how beautiful or realistic the image is.

## For each sample

1. **How well does the image match the text?** 1 = does not match, 5 = fully matches.
2. **Is there anything in the image that should not be there, given the text?** If yes, click on each element you mean.
3. **How sure are you about your answers?**
4. *(Optional)* A sentence explaining your answer, for example what made you lower your rating.

There are no right or wrong answers, except for a few attention checks. Answer with your first impression and do not go back to change earlier answers.

## Notes for the study team (not shown to annotators)

- Hide sample labels during real annotation (toggle “Show sample labels” in the interface).
- Randomize order per annotator. Stimuli that share a prompt or an image (`image_id`) should be split across annotators (between-subjects) or separated by filler items.
- Exclude annotators who rate the attention check (`E1`, sample `S12`) above 2.
- Keep inter-annotator disagreement as a signal; do not only average it away.
